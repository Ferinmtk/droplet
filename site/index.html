Share with people tooNew

Your deskmate’s phone? Pair it as someone else’s.

When you pair, Droplet asks whether the device is yours or someone else’s. A friend or colleague gets just enough to swap things with you, and nothing more.

  • They can send you files and messagesAnd you can send them yours, the same way you do between your own devices.
  • They never see your clipboard or notificationsThose stay between your own devices. They can’t control your computer either.
  • Pause anyone, any timeOne tap stops all sharing with them until you resume. Anything sent meanwhile simply waits.

Download

Get Droplet on every device

Free everywhere. Put it on two devices, pair them once, and you’re done.

Droplet for Android

Android 8 or newer · Free · No Google account needed

Download for Android

The app comes straight from our GitHub releases, with checksums.

  1. On your phone, tap Download for Android, then open the file.
  2. Your phone asks to allow installing apps from your browser. Allow it once, then tap Install.
  3. Open Droplet, tap Pair a device, pick your computer, and check both screens show the same four digits.

Then, in any app, tap Share → Droplet to send a photo or file to your computer.

Droplet for iPhone

iPhone & iPad · A web app, nothing to download from the App Store

Open Droplet for iPhone

Opened this from WhatsApp? Copy the link and paste it into Safari.

  1. Open droplet.noxeratech.com/app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. On your computer, open Droplet and choose Pair an iPhone.
  4. Scan the code it shows with your iPhone.

For now, an iPhone pairs with a Mac or Linux computer running Droplet, on the same Wi-Fi. Keep the app open while you send: iPhone pauses web apps in the background.

Droplet for Windows

Windows 10 & 11 · Free on the Microsoft Store

Get it fromMicrosoft

Listed as NOXERA Droplet. The Store keeps it up to date.

  1. Click Get it from Microsoft, then Get or Install in the Store.
  2. Open Droplet from the Start menu.
  3. On your phone, tap Pair a device and pick this PC. Accept on the PC when both show the same four digits.

Droplet for Mac

macOS · One command, no password needed

The installer checks everything it downloads. Read what it does first, if you like.

  1. Open Terminal: press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  2. Paste this and press Return:
    curl -fsSL https://droplet.noxeratech.com/mac.sh | sh
  3. If your Mac offers to install the Command Line Tools, say yes, then run the command again.
  4. Open Droplet. On your phone, tap Pair a device and pick this Mac.

Want your phone as a mouse and keyboard? Allow it once under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Nothing else needs it.

Droplet for Linux

Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian and more · No sudo needed

Needs Python 3.9 or newer. Read what the installer does.

  1. Open Terminal (Ctrl+Alt+T on most desktops).
  2. Paste this and press Enter:
    curl -fsSL https://droplet.noxeratech.com/linux.sh | sh
  3. Droplet appears in your app menu and system tray. On your phone, tap Pair a device and pick this computer.

On Debian or Ubuntu, if it asks for python3-venv, run sudo apt install python3-venv and paste the command again.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No ads, no subscription, no account. Droplet is open source under the GPL-3.0, so anyone can read the code.

Do I need internet?

No. Your devices talk over your Wi-Fi, or over one phone’s hotspot, with no internet and none of your data bundle used. You only need internet to download Droplet.

Is it safe? Who sees my files?

Only your devices. Files and messages go straight from one to the other, encrypted, and the four-digit code makes sure each device is talking to the right one. There’s no Droplet server, and nothing is collected. What it sends, and where.

Can I swap files with a friend?

Yes. Pair their device and choose Someone else’s. They can send you files and messages, but never see your clipboard or your notifications, and you can pause them any time.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, as a web app you add to your Home Screen from Safari. For now it pairs with a Mac or Linux computer running Droplet, and works while it’s open. How to set it up.

Why does Android ask about installing unknown apps?

Droplet isn’t on Google Play yet, so Android asks once before installing an app from your browser. Allow it for your browser, install, and you can switch it off again afterwards.

Does it work away from home?

Droplet is made for devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. If you use Tailscale, your devices can reach each other from anywhere, still directly.
